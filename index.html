questions

Questions you'll have before signing.

How fast can you launch?

Most installs go live in 5–10 business days. We handle the build, training, and deployment. You provide a phone number to forward, your booking calendar, and 30 minutes for a kickoff call.

Will the AI sound robotic?

No. We use Retell AI voices that pass the "is this a real person?" test. We tune the tone, pacing, and script to your business before going live.

What does it cost?

Retainers run $500–$997/month depending on call volume and the channels you need covered. No setup fees during our current launch pricing. We'll give you an exact number on the discovery call.

Are there contracts?

Month-to-month only. Cancel anytime with 30 days' notice. No long-term commitments.

What do I need to provide?

A phone number to forward, access to your booking calendar, and 30 minutes for an onboarding call. We build and manage everything else.

What industries do you work with?

Best fit: dental, med spas, HVAC and home services, chiro, law firms, and any local service business that fields inbound calls. If you're losing leads to voicemail, we can help.
